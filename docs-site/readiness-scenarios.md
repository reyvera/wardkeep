# Readiness comparisons

{: .fs-9 }

Explore one recorded household factor without changing your household record.
{: .fs-6 .fw-300 }

Wardkeep can compare your current readiness picture with a single explicit
change to a factor it already evaluates. The comparison is deterministic: it
uses the current recorded evidence, the exact change you enter, and the same
readiness calculation used on your Dashboard.

{: .note }
This is a comparison, not a prediction. Wardkeep does not forecast market,
employment, insurance, property, or other unrecorded outcomes.

## How to use it

1. Open **Dashboard** and choose **Compare readiness**.
2. Select a factor Wardkeep already evaluates.
3. Set the hypothetical effect of that factor, or compare the picture without
   it.
4. Review the current and comparison scores side by side, including the direct
   pillars and derived Peace.

The page always identifies the current recorded factor and the exact change
being compared. It does not hide uncertainty behind a single score: coverage
and any incomplete pillar remain visible in the result.

## What Wardkeep does not do

- It does not save a scenario or change any account, obligation, policy, asset,
  recommendation, or household-access record.
- It does not create a readiness-history snapshot or make the comparison look
  like a real change to the household.
- It does not create broad life-event presets such as “lose a job” or “buy a
  home,” because those outcomes would require assumptions that are not in your
  records.
- It does not make a financial, legal, insurance, or other professional
  recommendation.

## Why this boundary matters

Wardkeep's role is to explain the evidence you have recorded and show the
effect of a stated condition—not to claim certainty about the future. Future
comparison builders may cover clearly bounded topics such as a recorded cash
reserve, recurring obligation, or debt minimum. Each must show the source
records, assumptions, and limitations before presenting a result.
