# NameLab — Final Shortlist
*Product: Self-hosted, AI-powered personal finance*
*Themes: Homestead. Protect. Provide. Preside.*
*Aesthetic: Premium-minimal, dark, tool-like (Linear/Obsidian tier)*
---
## Tier 1 — Homestead Theme (self-sufficient, land-owning, your domain)
### Croft
- **Meaning:** A small self-sufficient farm. Worked by one family, owned outright.
- **Domain:** croft.com ✅
- **Score:** 7.96/10
- **Why:** Self-hosted = self-sufficient. Your financial croft — small, yours, productive. 5 chars, one syllable, strong.
- **CLI:** `$ croft sync` / `$ croft import`
- **Tagline:** *"Croft — Tend your own."*
- **Associations:** Self-reliance, land, ownership, Scottish highlands, quiet productivity.
### Garth
- **Meaning:** An enclosed yard or garden. A protected inner space.
- **Domain:** garth.com ✅
- **Score:** 7.60/10
- **Why:** Private, enclosed, yours. A garth is a courtyard — walled in, protected. 5 chars, one syllable.
- **CLI:** `$ garth sync` / `$ garth check`
- **Tagline:** *"Garth — Your enclosed ground."*
- **Associations:** Privacy, enclosure, garden, medieval, protected space.
### Steading
- **Meaning:** A farmstead. The buildings and land of a farm collectively.
- **Domain:** steading.com ✅
- **Score:** ~7.5/10
- **Why:** "Stead" = place you stand + "ing" = ongoing. Your financial steading. 8 chars, 2 syllables.
- **CLI:** `$ steading sync`
- **Tagline:** *"Steading — Where you stand."*
- **Concern:** 8 chars is slightly long. But .com is available.
### Glebe
- **Meaning:** A piece of land belonging to a parish. Land set aside for sustenance.
- **Domain:** glebe.dev
- **Score:** 7.77/10
- **Why:** Land allocated for provision. 5 chars, one syllable, uncommon.
- **CLI:** `$ glebe sync`
- **Concern:** Obscure. Most people don't know this word.

## Tier 2 — Protect/Preside Theme (strong, guardian, overseer)
### Aegis
- **Meaning:** A shield of protection (Zeus's shield). "Under the aegis of" = under protection.
- **Domain:** aegis.app ✅
- **Score:** 7.87/10
- **Why:** Protection of your finances/data. Classical, powerful, 5 chars.
- **CLI:** `$ aegis sync` / `$ aegis guard`
- **Tagline:** *"Aegis — Shield what matters."*
- **Concern:** Used by other companies (Aegis Authenticator, etc.). TM risk.
### Reeve
- **Meaning:** A medieval official who managed a lord's estate and finances.
- **Domain:** none available (.com/.app/.dev all taken)
- **Score:** 7.91/10
- **Why:** *Literally* the historical word for "the person who manages the money." Perfect semantic fit.
- **CLI:** `$ reeve sync` / `$ reeve import`
- **Tagline:** *"Reeve — Preside over your own."*
- **Concern:** No domain available. Would need getreeve.com or reeve.finance.
### Holdfast
- **Meaning:** A device that grips firmly. A command: "Hold fast!"
- **Domain:** holdfast.app ✅
- **Score:** ~7.5/10
- **Why:** Hold fast to your data. Hold fast to what you've built. 8 chars.
- **CLI:** `$ holdfast sync`
- **Tagline:** *"Holdfast — Grip what's yours."*
- **Concern:** 8 chars. Compound word.
### Dominion
- **Meaning:** Sovereignty and control over a territory.
- **Domain:** dominion.app ✅
- **Score:** ~7.3/10
- **Why:** Dominion over your data, your finances. Sovereign ownership.
- **CLI:** `$ dominion sync`
- **Tagline:** *"Dominion — Sovereign over your own."*
- **Concern:** 8 chars. Religious connotations. Game (Dominion).
