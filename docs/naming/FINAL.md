- A **warden** who guards something valuable
- A **keep** — the strongest, most defensible tower in a castle
- **Ward** — to protect, to watch over, to shelter
- **Keep** — to hold, to retain, to not let go
Together: *the guardian of the stronghold.* Your data, your finances, your rules — guarded.
